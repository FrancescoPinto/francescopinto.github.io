Biography

Building a safer foundation for AI.

Francesco Pinto researches the security, safety, and reliability of intelligent systems. His work connects rigorous machine-learning research with the practical challenges of deploying agents and multimodal models responsibly.

01

Biography

Francesco's research asks what it takes for AI systems to remain dependable beyond the laboratory. He develops methods to assess and strengthen systems facing uncertainty, shifting data, privacy constraints, and adversarial behaviour.

His recent work focuses on the safety and security of AI agents and multimodal models, with a broader interest in trustworthy machine learning. Across research and applied settings, he works on evaluation, red teaming, guardrails, robustness, calibration, and privacy-preserving learning.
